    OUR PRODUCTION PROCESS

    From sugarcane processing to quality jaggery products, every stage is handeled with care and consistency.

    01

    Sugarcane Selection

    Selection of suitable sugarcane for jaggery production.

    02

    Juice Extraction

    Sugarcane is processed to extract the juice for further production.

    03

    Extracted juice is filtered before further processing.
